# The Eight Movements
### From the First Aspiration to the Ocean-Seal Samādhi

The English edition of **《指月八部曲——從初發心到海印三昧》** by 釋慧鏡 (Shi Huijing).

> Yogācāra as the bone, the eight schools as the flesh, the modern world as the skin — one path of practice.

This is the accessible *book* layer, rendered into English from the Chinese edition and term-anchored to the bilingual scholarly corpus in this repository. It lives here at `book/en/`, a part-for-part parallel of the Chinese book under `book/`.

## The road

Eight parts, one road:

**Trust · Understand · Practice · Inquiry · Cutting · Illumination · Gathering-In · Manifestation**

Trust is the first step, because without trust there is no setting out at all. But trust is not the destination — it only pushes the door open.

## How this edition is made

Each chapter is translated from the approved Chinese chapter and term-anchored to its source paper, so that every doctrinal term uses the rendering the author has already settled. Sūtra and śāstra quotations reuse the verified English from the papers wherever they exist. The locked glossary is at [`book/BAIFA_ALIGNMENT.md`](../BAIFA_ALIGNMENT.md).
